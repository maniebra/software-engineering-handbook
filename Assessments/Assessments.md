# Assessments

Longer, mixed quizzes for checking whether the material actually stuck. Unlike the
short "Check Your Understanding" blocks at the end of each note, these pull questions
from a whole topic and shuffle them, so you cannot answer from the context of the
paragraph you just read.

Every question can be retried, and each correct answer carries an explanation.

## How to use these

- **Before reading a section**, to find out what you already know and what to focus on.
- **After reading it**, to check retention rather than recognition.
- **As exam practice**, since the questions are written in the style of a written exam
  rather than as recall prompts.

A wrong answer usually points at one page. The explanations name the concept, so search
the site for it rather than re-reading the whole section.
